# Public document references

Only metadata is distributed. No full articles, judgments, books, procurement documents or saved pages are included.

`terms.json` retains capture times, original/retrieval addresses and SHA-256 where the capture register has them. Its title is a descriptive document-layer label. Failed captures have no hash. Provider names identify subjects of the audit, not tools used for this deposit.

`digital.json` and `cross_sector.json` retain full citations and addresses with capture hashes where recorded. A capture hash authenticates retrieved bytes, not a successful substantive reading: an HTML block page can also have a hash. Use the episode reading-depth and source-check codes. Blank hashes identify missing capture metadata; dates from the original source register are labelled separately from later capture timestamps. Issuer/publication fields are citation attributions rather than newly verified corporate metadata.

`tenders.json` identifies the issuing buyer and original document address. Original document hashes and access dates are not in the inspected table and are explicitly unavailable. Separate hashes identify the retained text extractions only. Third-party documents remain with their issuers and are outside both deposit licences.

No sources were fetched or re-read for substantive claims during this packaging step.
