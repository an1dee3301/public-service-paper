## 1. Question, sample and admissible claim

For two deliberately selected post-deployment service episodes, can dated financing and final acceptance/activation powers be assigned to their actual holders and joined to subsequent usable service, price and resident burdens? **SASSA's 2018 payment transition is the anchor; the DeKUT/KENET renewal-delay account is the contrast.** Babyl and BHASHINI are secondary boundary evidence and do not enter this extraction.

The default output is a qualitative institutional diagnostic. These are different services, countries, dates and selection processes, with no common intervention, sampling denominator or common welfare scale. Do not pool costs, estimate prevalence, rank providers/countries, or interpret an outcome difference as a rights or ownership effect. A sufficiently documented within-case episode could later justify a separately specified causal study; this extraction does not create one.

The memo's proposed cells—SASSA as resident financing without acceptance, KENET as partial resident acceptance—are hypotheses to audit. Agency appropriations, judicial supervision, institutional membership, consultation and supplier-granted credit do not by themselves establish resident-held financing or final refusal power. Code missing records as **unknown**, not zero. Neither case has an established learned-AI component at the focal event; retain them as digital-service mechanism analogues.

The current model's `q` removes independent human review while incumbent service operates. Continuity, price and the harmful condition are separate observations. Do not map an outage to `q=1`, continued operation to `q=0`, or an affordable alternative to an independently met service floor.

## 2. Units, clocks and per-service floors

| Item | SASSA anchor | DeKUT/KENET contrast |
|---|---|---|
| Focal episode | Replacement arrangements signed 28 September 2018 before CPS service ended 30 September; October payment delivery, with separately dated 2019 access evidence | One renewal delay underlying the provider-hosted DeKUT testimony published 24 July 2026; exact due date and continuation interval must be recovered |
| Desired observation unit | Eligible beneficiary × scheduled grant month × location/channel | Student × scheduled Moodle assessment × deadline; conferencing is a separate service |
| Minimum task floor | Full statutory amount due is actually obtainable by the scheduled applicable payment date, through an authorized and usable channel; distinguish account credit from obtaining funds | Every scheduled student can authenticate, access the assessment, submit by the applicable deadline, and retain a durable acknowledged submission that can be retrieved for grading |
| Additional quality/burden measures | Charges, transport and repeat trips, travel/wait separately, disability accommodation, safety, recourse and remediated failures | Failed/non-login attempts, deadline extensions, lost submissions, accessibility, student connectivity/data cost, support and restoration time; grade/course completion distinct |
| Quantitative threshold rule | Use a dated applicable schedule/obligation where available; otherwise deadline compliance is unknown. Report access costs/time without inventing a maximum acceptable burden | Use the actual assessment rules and roster. Do not infer a deadline, allowable data loss or denominator from testimonial capacity |
| Substitute floor | Same due entitlement and timing, compatible identity/access and staffing/cash infrastructure; number of providers alone fails | Same assessment/records, authentication, workload, connectivity and support; a storage bucket, backup or generic VPS is not a running substitute |

The two floors serve the same conceptual purpose—successful completion of the service's essential resident task—but are **not numerically equivalent outputs**. Both lack user-ratified burden thresholds; this limits a claim of equivalent quality even if the essential task is observed.

Maintain separate clocks for signing, effective date, funding availability, decision/approval, payment due, actual service event, observation/fieldwork and publication. Later accounts may corroborate an earlier transaction; their publication cannot move a decision back in time. Publication of KENET's testimonial does not date its renewal episode. Record uncertainty as an interval only when the document supports both bounds.

## 3. Rights and temporal join rules

For each power record holder, represented population, legal/contractual instrument, scope, effective date, decision trigger and observed exercise. Record formal authority separately from ability to use it.

1. **Financing:** binding power to allocate usable cash/capacity to the alternative before the relevant offer or continuity decision. A resident's own bank selection is not authority over SASSA's infrastructure advance. Membership fees do not establish a student's choice over a university/KENET budget.
2. **Acceptance/refusal:** binding power to reject the specified supplier condition or amended terms, with the consequences and remedy documented. Complaints and participation are separate fields.
3. **Activation:** power and operational ability to initiate an available alternative after refusal, including necessary permissions, staff and contracts. A clause requiring another agreement is not an executed agreement or exercised step-in.
4. **Representation:** if power is delegated, identify how affected residents authorize and can hold that delegate to account; do not presume that any public agency or university stands in for the model's resident principal.

Use codes **documented holder**, **documented restriction/absence within a specified instrument**, **reported action without instrument**, and **unknown**. The second code is narrow: a contract allocating a particular decision to an agency establishes that allocation, not the absence of every resident remedy elsewhere.

A causal-order gate passes only when the relevant authority and usable resource/capacity are dated before the focal offer/decision and the defined resident outcome follows. Different fiscal years, undated statements and aggregate outcomes cannot be silently joined into an event-level record.

## 4. Accounting scope and outcomes

Keep three separate accounts, in original currency and stated prices/year: **payer cash flows**, **consolidated real service resources**, and **resident incidence**. No currency conversion or cross-case cost ratio is planned.

| Component | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Capital and setup | Record asset/service scope, acquisition amount/date, allocation and useful-life assumptions. Report capital spending and depreciation/use cost separately; do not add both to the same resource-cost total |
| Recurring operations | Staff, systems, compute, connectivity, cash logistics, security, support, maintenance and shared overhead; declare allocation and coverage before a total |
| Transfers and advances | Statutory grants are transfers to beneficiaries. SASSA–SAPO advance principal, setoff and later repayment are financing flows; eliminate within-public-sector transfers in consolidated resources |
| Fees/invoices | State covered quantity, period, tax and channel. A service fee can fund costs/profit; do not add it to the same operator's expenses as another resource cost without an incidence reconciliation |
| Credit | An overdue invoice is exposure; settlement is not a second service. Separate carrying cost, expected loss and any subsidy; no presumption these are zero |
| Household/student burdens | Report money, time and failure separately. No unelicited time valuation, invented harm score or subtraction of unrelated headline budgets |

At minimum record supplier/payer price or actual invoice separately from operation/availability, essential-task completion, and resident burdens/remedies. Finance recovered is not service obtained. Institutional platforms remaining online is not every assessment being completed. Do not compute net welfare without compatible benefit and incidence observations.

## 5. Rival explanations and disconfirmation

For SASSA retain closure/relocation selection, distance/bank density, urban/rural and disability needs, card/channel changes, staffing/cash/security constraints, fiscal cost shifting and changing beneficiary composition. For KENET retain ordinary administrative payment delay, pre-existing grace terms, institutional creditworthiness, demand/capacity changes, campus connectivity and assessment redesign. A commercially supplied equivalent credit/continuation arrangement is a live rival, not a presumed failed comparator.

Disconfirm the proposed resident-rights contrast if documents allocate the focal decisions elsewhere and no applicable resident instrument is recovered; report **contrast unestablished**, not a proven general absence of rights. Disconfirm an implication that agency-financed replacement automatically protects residents when successful agency finance coexists with documented access burdens. Report that coexistence without claiming finance caused the burdens. Disconfirm ownership-specific interpretation if equivalent rights/resources/costs explain the observation; the present data may simply leave that rival unresolved.

Produce one evidence matrix per case and a joined gate table with **pass / partial / not established / incompatible** and exact source locators. The following conditions govern conclusions:

- **R1 — resident authority:** do not assign the model's resident financing or acceptance cells without the applicable holder/instrument/timing evidence.
- **R2 — event order:** without a dated event and pre-event resources/authority, reject a before/after rights interpretation.
- **R3 — service floor:** without eligible-user denominator and actual task completion, do not call aggregate operation equivalent continuity.
- **R4 — cost scope:** without capital/recurring/transfer/household reconciliation, reject a full-cost or welfare comparison.
- **R5 — comparable contrast:** structurally different outputs and confounded settings preclude numerical pooling/ranking. Retain the purposive diagnostic even if within-case documents improve.
- **R6 — model mapping:** without an observed specified harmful condition, its applicable decision and resident outcome, reject a test of the current joint-rights proposition.

A completed extraction with failed gates is a completed **feasibility/diagnostic exercise**, not a completed causal rights test or a recovered dataset. Preserve that distinction in plan and tracker updates. Reopening requires a specific new document or authorized dataset capable of changing a named gate, rather than another general search.
