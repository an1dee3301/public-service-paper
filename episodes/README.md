# Episode data and limits

`digital/records.csv` contains the 27 original digital-system records; `cross_sector/records.csv` contains the 33 original cross-sector records. Each retains source links, coding limits and reading depth. The narrative insertion column is excluded. Source identities may be recoverable from public citations; these are not anonymised research participants.

These are the original documentary banks, not transcriptions of the separately posted 6 October manuscript tables. Later source checks corrected some locators and narrowed some claims in the paper. The accompanying `source_check_codes.csv` files preserve the independently checked verdicts, reading depth and corrected locators available at that checking stage; they are not a claim that every original bank cell was re-adjudicated. Final wording and later narrowing must be read in the paper. No manuscript cells have been copied into the deposit to conceal that distinction.

`transitions/design.md` preserves the frozen purposive design, including prior exposure and rejection rules. `transitions/evidence_matrix.md` preserves the subsequent named-lead matrix. These are diagnostic records, not a common causal sample. Public originals are referenced, not redistributed.
